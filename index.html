Real packs for two shops. Guided checklists for the rest.

A master file can be packed as a ZIP for Etsy and Creative Fabrica. Adobe Stock, Spoonflower and Canva need a different kind of file, so they get a step-by-step checklist — not an auto-generated pack.

Export pack

Etsy

A digital-download bundle — PDF, PNG and SVG — checked against Etsy’s file limits (up to 5 files, 20 MB each) and filename rules. Titles and tags are still yours to write.

Export pack

Creative Fabrica

The closest cousin of the Etsy pack: craft-ready SVG, PNG and PDF, plus a preview and a licence sheet. Whether a listing is a single sale or in the subscription library is a choice you record, not something the pack invents.

Guided checklist

Adobe Stock

Split the work into individual vectors, add titles and keywords, and confirm Adobe’s current illustration specs and generative-AI rules. A customer ZIP is not a stock submission.

Guided checklist

Spoonflower

Create and proof a seamless, high-resolution repeat. A motif pack is not a tiled fabric design, and MotifPass will not invent one from your master file.

Guided checklist

Canva

Build a template in the Canva editor. A downloadable SVG or PDF is a different product, and MotifPass cannot turn one into a Canva template for you.

A net calculator with a separate input for each site

There is no shared $10 model. Each marketplace has its own input, and every rate has a source link and a last-verified date.

  • Etsy and Creative Fabrica — a sale price. On Etsy, add an Offsite Ads toggle. On Creative Fabrica, choose a single sale or a subscription (subscription downloads pay from a pool, and that amount is unknown).
  • Adobe Stock — the buyer’s plan or licence type. You do not set a price. Official pay is per download by plan, not a percentage of a price you type.
  • Spoonflower — the product and the fabric price the customer pays. The royalty is a share of that printed-item price, not a digital download.
  • Canva — the royalty pool, shown as unknown per export. Dollars per export are unpublished. $10 here is the monthly payout threshold, not a price.

A Design Passport you keep yourself

You keep one record per design — owner, AI use, licence, exclusivity, and where it is listed. MotifPass does not scan live shops, and the log is record-keeping, not legal or tax advice.

Manual rights log

If you have marked a design as selling at full price on Etsy and also listed in Creative Fabrica’s subscription library, MotifPass warns you and suggests keeping distinct catalogues. The warning only knows what you entered. Payout and forfeiture reminders use sourced rules, with balances you enter by hand.

Account-level AI settings

Shop- or account-wide duties live in their own section, apart from each design’s checklist: Canva’s AI-training opt-out, Adobe’s generative-AI rules, and Etsy’s rule to disclose AI in a listing when it was used. The per-design checklist then applies those rules to one file.

Not legal or tax advice. The Design Passport is a record you maintain. It is not a scan of your live shops, and it is not legal, tax, or earnings advice.
